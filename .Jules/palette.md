## 2025-05-14 - Retrofitting Accessibility to Table-Based UIs
**Learning:** Legacy table-based layouts can be made accessible without a full rewrite by using a centralized DOMContentLoaded script to inject ARIA roles, tab indices, and keyboard listeners. This preserves the original structure while meeting modern standards.
**Action:** Use programmatic enhancement for legacy components to ensure accessibility when structural refactoring is out of scope or risky.
